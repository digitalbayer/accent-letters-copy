# Accent Letters: Copy & Paste

Copy accented A to Z Latin letters with one click. Private, fast and available offline.

## Description

Accent Letters gives you a compact A to Z reference directly in your browser toolbar. Choose a base letter, select the accented character you need, and paste it into a document, message, form or creative project.

The extension includes 488 precomposed Latin characters plus two clearly identified Q combining sequences. Characters are organized by their familiar A to Z base letter so they are quick to find.

### What it does

* Copies the exact selected character with one click
* Provides lowercase and uppercase forms
* Works without an internet connection
* Links to detailed Unicode references on DiacriticalMarks.com
* Supports mouse and keyboard use

### Privacy

The extension does not collect, store, sell or transmit personal information. It never reads your clipboard. It only writes the character you deliberately select.
